Privacy Policy for Super Calculator

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Super Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Super Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers. The application does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Some application settings or calculator-related information may be stored locally on your device to provide the application's features. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. Internet Connection

The current version of Super Calculator does not require an internet connection for its basic calculator functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

Super Calculator currently does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Super Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

6. Data Security

Because Super Calculator does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, there is no personal information stored by us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
